  • TF x is top flange x (width) in mm

  • BF x is bottom flange x (width) in mm

  • Web x is web x (thk) in mm
  • Web y is web y (height) in mm
  • TR x is top reinforcement x (width) in mm
  • TR y is top reinforcement y (thk) in mm

  • area is x-sec area of beam in mm2
  • weight is beam weight in kg
  • sb is bending stress in MPa
  • ss is shear stress in MPa
  • m def is deflection due to bending moment in mm
  • s def is deflection due to shear in mm
  • total def is in mm/m due to both shear and bending moment
