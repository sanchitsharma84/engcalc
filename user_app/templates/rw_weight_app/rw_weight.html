Filling instruction
  • It calculates only Plate and Round/Cylinder weight
  • If density is in kg/mm3, lengths in mm, then weight is in kg
  • If density is in kg/m3, lengths in m, then weight is in kg
  • In "Shape" column, select Plate for plate and Round for round shape
  • To subtract one shape from another, use negative values (-1, -2 etc) in Qty
  • OD is outer diameter of round. ID is the innder diameter of round if the shape is hollow
  • Input fields are common for Plate and Round shapes. Actual shape is determined by the Character P or R in Shape field
