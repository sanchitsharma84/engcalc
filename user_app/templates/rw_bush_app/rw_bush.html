Notes
  • This program calculates hydrodynamic bush
  • w is bush load in ton
  • d is bush nominal dia in mm
  • l is bush width in mm
  • clr is diameteral clearance in mm
  • rpm is shaft rpm
  • mu id dynamic viscosity. for ISO VG 150, it is 0.1275 Pa.s
  • Ecc ratio is eccentricity ratio
  • If ecc ratio is 0, shaft is concentric with bush
  • If ecc ratio is 1, shaft touches bush
  • Ecc ratio should not be more than 0.95 for stable and reliable bearing
  • Lesser the ecc ratio, better is the bearing
  • S is the Sommerfeld number. It is used to find value of Ecc ratio
  • phi is the attitude angle in deg
  • flow is the amount of oil required in bush considering load in full cycle
  • film thickness is in microns
  • for Ra 0.1 - 0.2: film thk > 2.5 micron
  • for Ra 0.2 - 0.4: film thk > 6.2 micron
  • for Ra 0.4 - 0.8: film thk > 12.5 micron
  • for Ra 0.8 - 1.6: film thk > 25 micron
  • for Ra 1.6 - 3.2: film thk > 50 micron
