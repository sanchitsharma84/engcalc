Filling instruction
  • All dimensions in mm, angles in degrees
  • Pressure angle is 20 degrees
  • m - normal module
  • helix - helix angle
  • zp - No. of teeth in pinion
  • xp - correction factor in pinion. It is generally positive in pinion
  • zg - No. of teeth in gear
  • xg - correction factor in gear. It is generally negative in gear
  • pcd_p - pcd of pinion
  • pcd_g - pcd of gear
  • w_pcd_p - working pcd of pinion
  • w_pcd_g - working pcd of gear
  • od_p - OD of pinion
  • od_g - OD of gear
  • root_d_p - Root dia of pinion
  • root_d_g - Root dia of gear
  • cd - center distance
